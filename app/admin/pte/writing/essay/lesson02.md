> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Exemplification（举例论证）

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!GOAL]
> Exemplification 是最常见、也是最容易掌握的论证方式。它通过具体事例来支持观点，使抽象的论点更加真实、具体且具有说服力。

> [!ANIMATE:typing]
> 一个好的例子能够回答：
>
> **Can you prove your opinion?**

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # 什么是 Exemplification？

:::columns ratio="45/55"

:::column

> [!ANIMATE:typing]
> **Definition**
>
> Exemplification means supporting a general idea with one or more specific examples.
>
> 它利用具体的人、事、现象或案例，证明一个观点是真实可信的。

:::

:::column

> # 什么情况下适合使用 Example？

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!KEYPOINT]
> 当观点比较抽象时，Example 往往是最直接、最有效的论证方式。

:::columns

:::column

> [!ANIMATE:typing]
> 常见话题：
>
> - Education
> - Technology
> - Environment
> - Health
> - Government

:::

> 例如：
>
> "Technology improves education."
>
> 这只是观点。
>
> 如果加入一个真实或合理的例子，观点就会更加具体。

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # 最常用的 Example 表达

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!KEYPOINT]
> 以下表达都可以用于引出例子，但正式程度略有不同。

| Expression | Formality | 用法 |
|------------|-----------|------|
| For example | ⭐⭐⭐ | 最常用，万能 |
| For instance | ⭐⭐⭐ | 与 For example 基本相同 |
| Such as | ⭐⭐ | 列举，不引导完整句 |
| Including | ⭐⭐ | 补充列举 |
| Like | ⭐ | 偏口语，不建议正式作文大量使用 |

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!TIP]
> PTE、IELTS 写作推荐优先使用 **For example** 和 **For instance**。

> - This can be seen in...

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Explanation（解释说明）

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!GOAL]
> Explanation 是学术写作中最重要的论证方式之一。它不仅告诉读者 **"是什么"**，更回答 **"为什么"** 和 **"如何理解"**。

> [!ANIMATE:typing]
> 一个好的 Explanation 能够把简单观点发展成为具有逻辑深度的论证。

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # 常用表达（Ⅰ）——重新解释

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!KEYPOINT]
> 当需要换一种方式解释时，可以使用以下表达。

| Expression | 用途 | 正式程度 |
|------------|------|---------|
| In other words | 换句话说 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| That is to say | 进一步解释 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| To put it another way | 换一种表达 | ⭐⭐⭐ |
| Put simply | 简单来说 | ⭐⭐ |
| Simply put | 简而言之 | ⭐⭐ |

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!TIP]
> **In other words** 最常见；**Simply put** 更适合降低理解难度。

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Cause & Effect（因果论证）

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!GOAL]
> Cause & Effect 是学术写作中最常见的论证方式之一。它通过分析原因（Cause）与结果（Effect）之间的关系，使文章更具逻辑性和说服力。

> [!ANIMATE:typing]
> 它回答的问题是：
>
> **Why does it happen?**
>
> **What will happen next?**

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # 什么是 Cause & Effect？

:::columns ratio="45/55"

:::column

> [!ANIMATE:typing]
> **Cause & Effect** 是利用原因和结果之间的逻辑关系来发展观点。
>
> 它不仅告诉读者**发生了什么**，更解释**为什么发生**以及**会带来什么影响**。

:::

:::column

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Step 1：Write a Topic Sentence

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!GOAL]
> 第一步，只提出观点。

> [!ANIMATE:typing]

Public transport should be improved in modern cities.

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!TIP]
> 这里只有观点。
>
> 不解释。
>
> 不举例。
>
> 不分析。

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Step 2：Ask "Why?"

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!GOAL]
> 第二步，不要急着举例。
>
> 先解释。

:::columns

:::column

> [!ANIMATE:typing]

Public transport should be improved in modern cities.

:::

:::column

> [!ANIMATE:typing]

because it can significantly reduce traffic congestion and improve travel efficiency.

:::

:::

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!KEYPOINT]
> 现在已经有了：
>
> Opinion + Explanation

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Step 3：Can You Prove It?

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!GOAL]
> 有了解释之后，再加入 Example。

:::columns

:::column

> [!ANIMATE:typing]

Public transport should be improved in modern cities because it can significantly reduce traffic congestion and improve travel efficiency.

:::

:::column

> [!ANIMATE:typing]

For example, cities such as Singapore and Tokyo have developed efficient railway systems that greatly reduce the number of private vehicles on the road.

:::

:::

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Step 4：Go One Step Further

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!GOAL]
> Example 已经证明观点了吗？
>
> 还不够。
>
> 继续分析影响。

:::columns

:::column

> [!ANIMATE:typing]

As a result,

less traffic congestion leads to lower carbon emissions and a more sustainable urban environment.

:::

:::column

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!TIP]

>
> 比单纯 Example 更有逻辑。

> # Let's Put Them Together

> [!ANIMATE:typing]

Public transport should be improved in modern cities because it can significantly reduce traffic congestion and improve travel efficiency.

For example, cities such as Singapore and Tokyo have developed efficient railway systems that greatly reduce the number of private vehicles on the road.

As a result, less traffic congestion leads to lower carbon emissions and a more sustainable urban environment.

Therefore, improving public transport is an effective way to build greener and more liveable cities.
