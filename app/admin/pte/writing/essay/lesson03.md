> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # What is PEEL?

```flow
theme: roadmap

Point

Explanation

Evidence / Example

Link
```

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

PEEL 是英语议论文中最经典的正文结构。

它告诉我们：

- **Point**：提出观点
- **Explanation**：解释观点
- **Evidence / Example**：提供支持
- **Link**：总结并扣题

> [!KEYPOINT]
> **PEEL 是段落结构，而不是论证方式。**

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # P — Point
>
> ## 提出观点

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

正文第一句话要明确告诉考官：

**这一段准备论证什么。**

> [!SUCCESS]
> Governments should invest more in public transport.

> [!TIP]
> 一个正文段，只讲**一个中心观点**。

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # E — Explanation
>
> ## 解释观点

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

Explanation 的作用就是回答：

**Why?**

为什么你的观点成立？

这一部分正是我们前面学习的**六种论证方式**发挥作用的地方。

例如：

- Explanation
- Cause & Effect
- Comparison
- Concession
- Classification
- Exemplification

> [!SUCCESS]
> This is because efficient public transport reduces traffic congestion and improves commuting efficiency.

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # E — Evidence / Example
>
> ## 提供支持

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

仅有解释还不够。

适当加入：

- 例子（Example）
- 事实（Fact）
- 数据（Statistics）
- 常识（General knowledge）

都可以增强说服力。

> [!SUCCESS]
> For example, Singapore's MRT system enables millions of people to travel efficiently every day.

> [!TIP]
> 一个简单、有代表性的例子即可。

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # L — Link
>
> ## 总结并扣题

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

最后一句重新连接主题。

提醒考官：

**所以，我的观点成立。**

> [!SUCCESS]
> Therefore, greater investment in public transport benefits both individuals and society.

> [!TIP]
> 不需要提出新观点，只需要回扣主题即可。

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # A Complete PEEL Paragraph

> [!NOTE]

**Point**

Governments should invest more in public transport.

↓

**Explanation**

This is because efficient public transport reduces traffic congestion and improves commuting efficiency.

↓

**Evidence**

For example, Singapore's MRT system enables millions of commuters to travel efficiently every day.

↓

**Link**

Therefore, investing in public transport benefits both individuals and society.

> # Where Do Argumentative Devices Fit?

```flow
theme: roadmap

Point

↓

Explanation
(Argumentative Devices)

↓

Evidence

↓

Link
```

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

前面学习的六种论证方式，

主要放在 **Explanation** 这一部分。

例如：

- Cause & Effect
- Comparison & Contrast
- Concession & Refutation
- Classification
- Explanation
- Exemplification

> [!KEYPOINT]
> **PEEL 决定结构，Argumentative Devices 决定如何展开论证。**

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Final Advice

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!SUCCESS]
> 一篇优秀的正文，不是写得越长越好。

而是：

```flow
theme: roadmap

One Point

↓

Clear Explanation

↓

Strong Support

↓

Logical Link
```

> [!KEYPOINT]
> **清晰的结构，比复杂的句子更重要。**
