> ## 单线图（Single Line Graph）

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Band 7 Template**
>
> The line graph provides information about 【标题】 over the period from 【起始时间】 to 【结束时间】.
>
> Overall, 【指标】 demonstrates a noticeable 【上升/下降/波动/稳定】 trend throughout the given period.
>
> At the initial stage, the figure stood at 【起始数值】. It then 【rose/fell/fluctuated】 steadily before reaching 【最高值/最低值】 in 【时间】.
>
> By the end of the period, the figure had reached 【结束数值】, indicating a significant change in 【标题】.
>
> In conclusion, the graph highlights a clear trend, and 【指标】 appears to be an important feature of the data.
