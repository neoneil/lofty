> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # What Is RTS?
>
> ## Respond to a Situation

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

RTS 是一项**情境口语任务**。

你需要：

- 理解自己扮演的角色
- 判断正在与谁交流
- 找出需要完成的任务
- 使用自然、完整的英语作出回应

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!KEYPOINT]
> ==RTS 不是写 Essay，也不是朗读正式邮件，而是在真实情境中直接与对方交流。==

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Think Before You Speak

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

回答之前，快速确认四个问题：

```flow
theme: roadmap

Who am I?

Who am I speaking to?

What happened?

What should I say or do?
```

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!TIP]
> 不要看到题目后立即套模板。
>
> 先判断角色、对象、问题和任务，再选择合适的表达。

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # RTS Is Spoken English

:::columns

:::column

### Avoid Writing Style

❌ I am writing to formally express my dissatisfaction regarding the issue.

❌ I would like to bring this matter to your immediate attention.

❌ I look forward to receiving a satisfactory resolution.

:::

:::column

### Use Spoken English

✅ Hi, I’d like to talk about a problem.

✅ The main issue is that...

✅ Could you please help me with this?

:::

:::

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!KEYPOINT]
> RTS 可以保持礼貌和正式，但表达必须听起来像真实交流，而不是一封被朗读出来的正式邮件。

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # The Four-part RTS Structure

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

```flow
theme: roadmap

Opening

Situation

Main Response

Closing
```

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]

四个部分分别完成不同任务：

1. **Opening** — 自然开始交流
2. **Situation** — 说明情况或问题
3. **Main Response** — 提出请求、建议、解决方案或其他回应
4. **Closing** — 礼貌结束

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Part 1
>
> ## Opening

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

Opening 的作用是自然进入情境。

常用表达：

- Hi, do you have a moment?
- Hi, I’d like to talk to you about something.
- Excuse me, could I speak with you for a moment?
- Hi, I just wanted to let you know about something.
- Hello, I was wondering if you could help me with something.

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!TIP]
> 根据交流对象调整语气。
>
> 对朋友可以更自然；对老师、经理或工作人员需要更礼貌。

left: Created by Chi - Lofty

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Choose the Right Opening

:::columns

:::column

### Friend or Classmate

- Hi, do you have a moment?
- Hey, can I talk to you about something?
- I just wanted to ask you something.

:::

:::column

### Teacher or Staff Member

- Excuse me, could I speak with you for a moment?
- I was wondering if you could help me with something.
- I’d like to discuss a problem with you.

:::

:::

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!KEYPOINT]
> 同一个 RTS 模板不能完全忽略人物关系，因为交流对象会影响语气和礼貌程度。

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Part 2
>
> ## Explain the Situation

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

用一到两句话说明发生了什么。

常用表达：

- The situation is that 【情况】.
- The main issue is that 【问题】.
- Unfortunately, 【问题】.
- I’ve recently noticed that 【问题】.
- I’m having some difficulty with 【问题】.
- There seems to be a problem with 【事物】.

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!TIP]
> 直接说明重点，不需要重复题目的全部背景。

left: Created by Chi - Lofty

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Situation Example

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Situation**
>
> You are working on a group assignment, but one group member has repeatedly missed meetings.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]

> [!SUCCESS]
> **Natural Spoken Response**
>
> Hi, do you have a moment? I’d like to talk about our group assignment. The main issue is that you’ve missed the last few meetings, so the rest of the group has found it difficult to organise the project.

---

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!KEYPOINT]
> 先客观说明情况，不要一开始就指责或使用过于情绪化的语言。

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Part 3
>
> ## Give the Main Response

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

这是 RTS 最重要的部分。

根据题目，你可能需要：

:::columns

:::column

### Common Tasks

- Make a request
- Ask for help
- Make a complaint
- Give advice
- Make a suggestion

:::

:::column

### Other Tasks

- Apologise
- Refuse politely
- Persuade someone
- Resolve a problem
- Explain a decision

:::

:::

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!KEYPOINT]
> ==你的回答必须真正完成题目要求，而不是只描述发生了什么。==

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Useful Response Patterns

:::columns

:::column

### Request

Could you please 【请求】?

Would it be possible to 【请求】?

### Suggestion

Perhaps we could 【建议】.

I think it would be better to 【建议】.

:::

:::column

### Advice

I think you should 【建议】.

It might be helpful to 【建议】.

### Solution

Maybe we could 【解决方案】.

One possible solution would be to 【解决方案】.

:::

:::

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Add a Reason

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

提出请求或建议后，可以简单解释原因。

常用表达：

- because 【原因】
- since 【原因】
- so that 【目的】
- This would help us 【好处】.
- That way, we could 【结果】.
- This would make it easier to 【好处】.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]

> [!SUCCESS]
> Could you please attend the next meeting **so that we can divide the remaining tasks fairly and finish the project on time?**

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!TIP]
> 一句清楚的理由就足够，不需要像 Essay 一样展开长篇论证。

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Part 4
>
> ## Polite Closing

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

用一句自然表达结束回应。

常用表达：

- I’d really appreciate your help.
- I hope we can work this out.
- Please let me know what you think.
- I hope you’ll consider my suggestion.
- Thanks for your understanding.
- Thank you for taking the time to listen.
- I’m sure we can find a suitable solution.

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!TIP]
> Closing 不需要太长，一句话即可自然结束。

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Universal RTS Template

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Spoken Template**
>
> Hi, 【称呼或自然开场】. I’d like to talk to you about 【话题】.
>
> The main issue is that 【情况或问题】, and this has 【影响】.
>
> Would it be possible to 【请求或解决方案】? I think this would help 【原因或好处】.
>
> I’d really appreciate your help, and I hope we can work this out. Thank you.

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!KEYPOINT]
> 这是一个通用结构，不是要求逐字背诵。考试时应根据角色、场景和任务自然调整。

> # Apply the Template

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Situation**
>
> The air conditioner in your university classroom has not been working. The room is becoming uncomfortable, and students are finding it difficult to concentrate. Speak to a university staff member and ask for help.

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Sample Response

> [!ANIMATE:typing]
> Excuse me, could I speak with you for a moment? I’d like to let you know about a problem with the air conditioner in our classroom. It hasn’t been working properly, and the room has become quite uncomfortable, making it difficult for students to concentrate. Could you please arrange for someone to check or repair it as soon as possible? This would help create a much more comfortable learning environment. I’d really appreciate your help. Thank you.

:::column

### Structure

✅ Natural opening

✅ Clear situation

✅ Direct request

✅ Relevant reason

✅ Polite closing

:::column

### Spoken Language

✅ I’d like to...

✅ It hasn’t been...

✅ Could you please...?

✅ I’d really appreciate...

:::

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Common Mistakes

:::columns

:::column

### Avoid

❌ Sounding like an email

❌ Using Essay introductions

❌ Repeating the entire prompt

❌ Explaining without responding

❌ Using very long sentences

:::

:::column

### Do This Instead

✅ Speak naturally

✅ Identify the task

✅ Respond directly

✅ Give a brief reason

✅ Finish politely

:::

:::

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Final RTS Checklist

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

回答之前：

- ☑ Who am I?
- ☑ Who am I speaking to?
- ☑ What happened?
- ☑ What do I need to do?

回答过程中：

- ☑ Start naturally
- ☑ Explain the situation clearly
- ☑ Complete the required task
- ☑ Give a brief reason
- ☑ Finish politely

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!KEYPOINT]
> ==A strong RTS response sounds like a real conversation: clear, direct, polite and natural.==
