# Line Chart 真正在考什么？

线图不是让你逐个数字翻译，而是考你是否能看出：

- 哪些项目上升
- 哪些项目下降
- 哪些阶段发生转折
- 最高点、最低点和明显差距在哪里

> [!KEYPOINT]
> ==先看整体趋势，再选关键数据。不要从左到右机械报数。==

# 四段结构

```flow
theme: business

Introduction

Overview

Body 1: main trend

Body 2: contrast or later trend
```

> [!TIP]
> Overview 必须回答“整体发生了什么”，不要写具体小数字。

# Introduction 改写题目

> [!EXAMPLE]
> The line graph compares the percentage of people using three different forms of transport between 1980 and 2020.

可替换表达：

- shows -> compares / illustrates / presents
- changes in -> trends in / variations in
- from 1980 to 2020 -> over the period shown / during the forty-year period

> [!COMMON-MISTAKE]
> 不要写成 `The line graph shows us...`。Task 1 学术写作中不需要 `us`。

# Overview 写法

Overview 重点写大趋势：

> [!EXAMPLE]
> Overall, car use increased steadily and remained the dominant category, while bus travel declined throughout the period. Train use fluctuated slightly before rising sharply in the final decade.

> [!KEYPOINT]
> Overview 不需要写所有数字，但必须覆盖主要方向和明显对比。

# 上升与下降

| 中文功能 | 高分表达 |
| --- | --- |
| 稳步上升 | increased steadily / rose gradually |
| 快速上升 | climbed sharply / saw a dramatic rise |
| 轻微下降 | fell slightly / declined marginally |
| 大幅下降 | dropped significantly / plunged |

> [!EXAMPLE]
> Oil consumption grew steadily from 1980 to 2000, apart from a slight drop in 1990.

# 最高点、最低点和平台期

| 功能 | 句型 |
| --- | --- |
| 达到最高点 | reached a peak of ... |
| 降到最低点 | fell to its lowest point at ... |
| 保持稳定 | remained stable at around ... |
| 小范围波动 | fluctuated within a narrow range |

> [!EXAMPLE]
> The unemployment rate stabilized at around 8% after several years of fluctuation.

> [!NOTE]
> `peak` 是名词也可以作动词：`The figure peaked at 9% in 1999.`

# 时间连接

写线图时，时间逻辑决定文章是否清楚。

- at the beginning of the period
- by 1990
- during the following decade
- after a brief decline
- by the end of the period

> [!EXAMPLE]
> The figure dipped to 12.3 million in 2001, before increasing substantially over the following three years.

# Body 段落写法

> [!KEYPOINT]
> 一段负责一组趋势，不要一段写完所有线。

Body 1 可以写：

- 起点最高或最低的线
- 上升最明显的线
- 主要类别

Body 2 可以写：

- 对比类别
- 下降线
- 波动线

# 高分句型组合

> [!EXAMPLE]
> The proportion of students choosing science rose from 42% to just over 50%, whereas the figure for arts declined gradually and finished at approximately 20%.

这句话包含：

- `rose from ... to ...`
- `whereas` 对比
- `finished at` 结尾数据
- `approximately` 避免过度精确

> [!TIP]
> 线图高分句不是长，而是信息组合得稳。

# 常见错误

> [!COMMON-MISTAKE]
> - 每一个年份都写，文章像流水账
> - Overview 写具体数字，没有总结
> - `increase` 和 `decrease` 词性混用
> - 忘记比较两条线之间的差距
> - 时态不统一

{red}错误：The number was increase.{/red}

{green}正确：The number increased. / There was an increase in the number.{/green}

# Line Chart Checklist

> [!SUMMARY]
> 写线图时检查：
>
> - [ ] Introduction 是否改写题目
> - [ ] Overview 是否总结主要趋势
> - [ ] Body 是否按趋势分组
> - [ ] 是否写了起点、终点、峰值或转折点
> - [ ] 是否有比较词：while / whereas / compared with

> [!HOMEWORK]
> 选择一张线图，用本节课句型写一篇 150-180 词 Task 1。完成后标出所有趋势动词。
