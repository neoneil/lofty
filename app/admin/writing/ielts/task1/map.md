# Map 题型分类

地图题通常分三类：

- 静态位置图：描述地点和方位
- 变化发展图：比较过去和现在
- 选址图：比较两个可能地点

> [!KEYPOINT]
> ==地图题的核心不是数字，而是空间关系。==

# Introduction

> [!EXAMPLE]
> The maps compare the layout of a village in 1990 with its current layout.

> [!EXAMPLE]
> The map shows two possible sites for a new supermarket in the town of Garlsdon.

可替换：

- show the layout of ...
- compare changes in ...
- illustrate two proposed locations for ...

# Overview

地图 Overview 写大变化：

- 哪些区域被开发
- 哪些设施被替换
- 城市是否扩张
- 哪个选址更靠近中心/交通线

> [!EXAMPLE]
> Overall, the village expanded considerably, especially towards the east, while the original central area remained largely unchanged.

# 方位表达

| 中文 | 英文 |
| --- | --- |
| 在东部 | in the east of |
| 在东边但不接壤 | to the east of |
| 接壤 | on the east of |
| 在西北 12 公里处 | 12 km to the north-west of |

> [!NOTE]
> `in` 表示在范围内，`to` 表示在范围外，`on` 表示接壤。

# 位置关系

好用表达：

- parallel to the river
- directly opposite the bus stop
- surrounded by trees
- in the top left-hand corner
- roughly in the middle
- along the roadside
- halfway between A and B

> [!EXAMPLE]
> The supermarket is located halfway between the railway station and the main road.

# 地图变化动词

| 变化 | 表达 |
| --- | --- |
| 扩大 | was enlarged / was extended |
| 缩小 | was reduced |
| 消失 | disappeared / was removed |
| 被改成 | was converted into |
| 被取代 | was replaced by / gave way to |
| 新建 | was constructed / was built |

> [!EXAMPLE]
> The farmland was replaced by a residential area, while the original road was extended to the east.

# 发展型地图

发展型地图要按时间写：

- during the first stage
- from 1883 to 1922
- during the following period
- by 1994

> [!EXAMPLE]
> From 1883 to 1922, the village expanded southwards, and a railway line was built across the new area.

> [!KEYPOINT]
> 发展型地图可以像时间线一样写，但重点仍然是空间扩张。

# 选址型地图

选址型地图常比较：

- 离市中心远近
- 是否靠近主路/铁路
- 是否方便停车
- 是否服务周边城镇
- 是否有交通限制

> [!EXAMPLE]
> S1 is outside the town centre and close to the main road, which would make it accessible to shoppers travelling by car.

# 常见错误

> [!COMMON-MISTAKE]
> - `in / to / on` 混用
> - 只写变化，不写方位
> - 写成主观评价文章
> - 忘记 Overview
> - 选址题没有比较优缺点

{red}错误：The school is in the north of the town, but actually it is outside the town.{/red}

{green}正确：The school is to the north of the town.{/green}

# Map Checklist

> [!SUMMARY]
> 写地图题时检查：
>
> - [ ] 是否说明地图类型
> - [ ] 是否写出整体变化或整体布局
> - [ ] 是否使用方位词
> - [ ] 是否写出新增、消失、替换
> - [ ] 选址题是否比较交通和位置

> [!HOMEWORK]
> 找一张地图题，先列出 8 个方位表达，再写一段 Overview。选址题额外写出每个位置的一个优点和一个缺点。
