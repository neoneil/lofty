# Bar Chart 的核心

柱图通常考两件事：

- 不同类别之间的比较
- 同一类别在不同时间或群体中的差异

> [!KEYPOINT]
> ==柱图不是逐项列数字，而是先排序，再比较。==

# 读图顺序

```flow
theme: business

Read title and unit

Find highest and lowest

Group similar categories

Choose key figures
```

> [!TIP]
> 先找“谁最大、谁最小、谁接近”，再决定 Body 怎么分段。

# Overview 写什么？

Overview 写总规律：

> [!EXAMPLE]
> Overall, practical crafts was the most popular subject area, while arts attracted the smallest proportion of students. Science and engineering showed broadly similar levels.

可以写：

- 最大类别
- 最小类别
- 是否有明显分组
- 是否有男女/国家/年份差异

# 排名表达

| 功能 | 表达 |
| --- | --- |
| 排第一 | ranked first / was the leading category |
| 紧随其后 | was closely followed by |
| 排第二 | came second / ranked second |
| 垫底 | had the lowest figure |

> [!EXAMPLE]
> Japan led the countries in life expectancy, with Australia and Canada following behind.

> [!NOTE]
> `followed by` 很适合写柱图排名，清楚且简洁。

# 比较表达

常见结构：

- A was higher than B.
- A was twice as high as B.
- A was marginally larger than B.
- A far exceeded B.
- A was in marked contrast to B.

> [!EXAMPLE]
> The proportion of homeowners was as high as 78%, in marked contrast to a mere 35% a decade earlier.

# 倍数表达

| 中文 | 英文 |
| --- | --- |
| 是两倍 | was twice as high as |
| 是三倍多 | was more than three times as high as |
| 翻了一倍多 | more than doubled |
| 多 50% | was nearly 50% higher than |

> [!EXAMPLE]
> Rent as a share of household expenditure more than doubled, rising from 5% to 12%.

> [!COMMON-MISTAKE]
> 不要写 `two times higher than` 来表示“两倍”。更稳的是 `twice as high as`。

# 占比表达

| 表达 | 用法 |
| --- | --- |
| account for | 占据比例 |
| make up | 构成 |
| constitute | 正式表达“组成” |
| comprise | 组成，占据 |

> [!EXAMPLE]
> Women made up 52% of the population, while men accounted for the remaining 48%.

> [!TIP]
> 饼图和柱图都可以大量使用 `account for` 与 `make up`。

# Body 分段方法

:::columns ratio="45/55"

:::column

> [!KEYPOINT]
> Body 1 写高值组：
>
> - 最大值
> - 第二大
> - 接近的类别

:::

:::column

> [!KEYPOINT]
> Body 2 写低值组：
>
> - 最小值
> - 明显落后的类别
> - 特殊对比

# 高分句型

> [!EXAMPLE]
> Students made up the biggest proportion of employees in low-paid occupations, while high school graduates were more likely to work in professional roles.

> [!EXAMPLE]
> The figure for China was far below that for the United States, although it was still higher than the figures recorded in several European countries.

> [!NOTE]
> 柱图的句子重点是比较，不是复杂从句越多越好。

# 常见错误

> [!COMMON-MISTAKE]
> - 忘记单位：percent / million / tonnes
> - 最大值和最小值没有写
> - 把所有类别平均分段
> - 只写数字，不写比较关系
> - Overview 写成细节段

{red}错误：The chart has many categories and they are different.{/red}

{green}正确：Overall, two categories dominated the chart, while the remaining groups recorded much lower figures.{/green}

# Bar Chart Checklist

> [!SUMMARY]
> 写柱图时检查：
>
> - [ ] 是否找出最高值和最低值
> - [ ] 是否把相似类别分组
> - [ ] 是否使用比较句型
> - [ ] 是否避免逐项报数
> - [ ] 是否写清楚单位和时间

> [!HOMEWORK]
> 找一张柱图，先写出三句话：最高值、最低值、一个明显对比。然后再扩展成完整文章。
