# Pie Chart 的核心

饼图考的是“整体里面各部分怎么分配”。

你需要关注：

- 最大板块
- 最小板块
- 两个板块合起来是否过半
- 是否有明显变化或替代

> [!KEYPOINT]
> ==饼图的关键词是 composition，不是 trend。==

# Introduction

> [!EXAMPLE]
> The pie charts compare how household spending was distributed across five categories in 1990 and 2020.

可替换：

- compare the proportions of ...
- show the breakdown of ...
- illustrate the distribution of ...

> [!NOTE]
> `breakdown` 很适合表示“构成明细”。

# Overview

Overview 通常写：

- 哪个类别最大
- 哪个类别最小
- 结构是否发生明显变化

> [!EXAMPLE]
> Overall, housing took up the largest share in both years, while entertainment remained the smallest category. The most noticeable change was the growing importance of transport.

# 占比动词

| 表达 | 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| account for | Food accounted for 35% of total spending. |
| make up | Rent made up nearly half of the budget. |
| constitute | Volunteers constituted nearly half of the workforce. |
| comprise | Construction waste comprised 40% of landfill. |

> [!TIP]
> 不要所有句子都用 `was`。饼图要主动使用占比动词。

# 分数表达

| 数字 | 表达 |
| --- | --- |
| 25% | a quarter |
| 20% | one fifth |
| 33% | about one third |
| 40% | two fifths |
| 50%+ | more than half |

> [!EXAMPLE]
> Less than a fifth of public money went to education.

> [!KEYPOINT]
> 分数表达能让文章更自然，但不要牺牲准确性。

# 两张饼图怎么写？

如果有两个年份：

- Body 1 写第一年主要结构
- Body 2 写第二年变化和对比

如果有两个国家：

- Body 1 写共同点
- Body 2 写差异点

> [!EXAMPLE]
> In 1990, food was the largest category, accounting for 40%. By 2020, however, its share had fallen to 25%, while transport had become much more significant.

# 合并信息

> [!EXAMPLE]
> Together, housing and food represented nearly two thirds of total expenditure, leaving the remaining categories with much smaller shares.

好用句型：

- together, A and B accounted for ...
- the remaining categories made up ...
- the rest was divided between ...
- A alone represented ...

> [!NOTE]
> 合并信息是饼图高分关键，因为它证明你在分析结构。

# 常见错误

> [!COMMON-MISTAKE]
> - 每个扇区都单独写一句
> - 忘记说明“整体”
> - 使用太多精确数字，句子很碎
> - `percentage` 和 `proportion` 搭配错误
> - 不会合并小类别

{red}错误：The percentage of food was 35%. The percentage of rent was 30%.{/red}

{green}正确：Food and rent together accounted for nearly two thirds of total spending.{/green}

# Pie Chart Checklist

> [!SUMMARY]
> 写饼图时检查：
>
> - [ ] 是否写出最大板块
> - [ ] 是否写出最小板块
> - [ ] 是否合并相关类别
> - [ ] 是否使用 account for / make up
> - [ ] 是否避免逐个扇区机械描述

> [!HOMEWORK]
> 找一组饼图，只用 4 句话完成 Overview 和 Body 1：最大、最小、合并、变化。
