# Process Diagram 的特点

流程图通常没有很多数字，核心是：

- 过程从哪里开始
- 有几个主要阶段
- 每一步发生什么
- 最后得到什么结果

> [!KEYPOINT]
> ==流程图写作不是比较，而是清楚说明 sequence。==

# 四个要领

```flow
theme: business

Use present tense

Use sequence words

Use passive voice

Describe every key stage
```

> [!NOTE]
> 如果题目是自然循环，常用一般现在时；如果是人工生产流程，常用被动语态。

# Introduction

> [!EXAMPLE]
> The diagram illustrates the process by which glass bottles are recycled.

更多开头：

- The diagram shows how cement is produced.
- The pictures demonstrate the stages in the life cycle of a silkworm.
- The diagram explains how water can be obtained outdoors.

> [!TIP]
> Introduction 只需要说明“什么过程”。不要提前写细节。

# Overview

流程图 Overview 写：

- 总共几个阶段
- 从什么开始
- 以什么结束
- 是否是循环

> [!EXAMPLE]
> Overall, the process consists of three main stages, beginning with the collection of used bottles and ending with new bottles being delivered to supermarkets.

> [!KEYPOINT]
> Overview 不能省。流程图也必须有总览。

# 顺序连接词

| 阶段 | 表达 |
| --- | --- |
| 开始 | first of all / to begin with / at the initial stage |
| 下一步 | next / then / subsequently / in the following stage |
| 同时 | at the same time / simultaneously |
| 之后 | after this / once this is done |
| 最后 | finally / in the final stage |

> [!EXAMPLE]
> Once the bottles have been washed, they are transported to a glass factory.

# 被动语态

人工流程常用被动：

- bottles are collected
- materials are crushed
- the mixture is heated
- the product is transported
- the final goods are packed

> [!EXAMPLE]
> Limestone and clay are placed on two separate conveyors and then crushed into powder.

{blue}结构：be + past participle{/blue}

# 时间和持续时间

有些流程图包含时间：

- It takes ten days for each egg to become a larva.
- This stage lasts for up to six weeks.
- After a period of about three weeks, the adult moth emerges.

> [!NOTE]
> 自然生命周期题常出现 `takes`、`lasts`、`after a period of`。

# 范例：玻璃回收

> [!EXAMPLE]
> In the first stage, used bottles are collected at recycling points and transported by truck. They are then sorted according to colour and washed with high-pressure water before being sent to a glass factory.

这段包含：

- 阶段连接：`In the first stage`
- 被动语态：`are collected`, `are sorted`
- 工具方式：`by truck`, `with high-pressure water`

# 范例：水的获取

> [!EXAMPLE]
> The hole is first covered with a plastic sheet, which is held in place by stones. As the sun heats the plants below, water evaporates and later condenses on the underside of the plastic.

> [!KEYPOINT]
> 自然过程可以混合主动与被动：自然变化用主动，人工操作用被动。

# 常见错误

> [!COMMON-MISTAKE]
> - 用过去时描述流程
> - 缺少 Overview
> - 只写箭头，不写动作
> - 被动语态错误
> - 漏掉图中重要步骤

{red}错误：The bottles collected and wash.{/red}

{green}正确：The bottles are collected and washed.{/green}

# Process Checklist

> [!SUMMARY]
> 写流程图时检查：
>
> - [ ] 是否说明过程目的
> - [ ] 是否写出开始和结束
> - [ ] 是否覆盖主要步骤
> - [ ] 是否使用顺序连接词
> - [ ] 被动语态是否准确

> [!HOMEWORK]
> 选择一个生产流程或自然循环图，先用中文列出 6 个步骤，再用被动语态写成英文段落。
