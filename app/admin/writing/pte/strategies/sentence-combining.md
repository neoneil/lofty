> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Why Sentence Combining?

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

PTE **Summarize Written Text** 的目标不是简单复制原文，而是：

- 提取核心信息
- 合并多个观点
- 保持逻辑连贯
- 使用正式的 Academic English

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!KEYPOINT]
> ==高分 SWT 的核心能力，不是写更多，而是把更多信息自然地写进同一句话。==

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Technique 1
>
> ## FANBOYS

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!KEYPOINT]
> FANBOYS 用于连接两个**地位相同**的完整信息，使句子更加流畅，同时避免重复使用多个短句。

:::columns

:::column

### FANBOYS

- For
- And
- Nor
- But
- Or
- Yet
- So

:::

:::column

### Most Common

- and
- but
- so
- yet

:::

:::

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # FANBOYS Example

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Original Sentences**
>
> Rapid technological innovation has transformed the way people communicate.
>
> It has also significantly increased the efficiency of international business and education.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]

> [!SUCCESS]
> **Academic Combination**
>
> Rapid technological innovation has transformed the way people communicate, **and** it has also significantly increased the efficiency of international business and education.

---

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]
> [!TIP]
> FANBOYS 最适用于连接两个**同等重要**的信息，而不是表示复杂逻辑关系。

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Technique 2
>
> ## Semicolon (;)

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!KEYPOINT]
> 分号用于连接两个**密切相关的完整句子**，比句号联系更紧密，也比 and 更正式，因此非常适合 Academic Writing。

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Semicolon Example

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Original Sentences**
>
> Governments are investing heavily in renewable energy.
>
> Many countries have successfully reduced their dependence on fossil fuels.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]

> [!SUCCESS]
> **Academic Combination**
>
> Governments are investing heavily in renewable energy; consequently, many countries have successfully reduced their dependence on fossil fuels.

---

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Another Example**
>
> Climate change poses serious environmental challenges; therefore, international cooperation has become increasingly important.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!TIP]
> 分号后通常接：
>
> - therefore
> - consequently
> - moreover
> - nevertheless
> - however

这样比直接使用句号更加符合 Academic Writing 的表达习惯。

> # Technique 3
>
> ## Relative Clause

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!KEYPOINT]
> **Relative Clause（定语从句）** 用来修饰名词，使多个句子自然合并，同时避免重复主语，是 SWT 中使用频率最高的句型之一。

:::columns

:::column

### Relative Pronouns

- who
- whom
- whose
- which
- that

:::

:::column

### Common Use

修饰：

- people
- things
- ideas
- organisations
- technologies

:::

:::

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Relative Clause Example

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Original Sentences**
>
> Artificial intelligence is developing rapidly.
>
> Artificial intelligence is transforming modern healthcare.
>
> Artificial intelligence is improving diagnostic accuracy.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]

> [!SUCCESS]
> **Academic Combination**
>
> Artificial intelligence, **which is developing rapidly**, is transforming modern healthcare by improving diagnostic accuracy.

---

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Another Example**
>
> Universities, **which play a vital role in scientific research**, contribute significantly to technological innovation and economic development.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!TIP]
> Relative clauses are ideal for adding **background information** without creating another sentence.

> # Technique 4
>
> ## Appositive Clause

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!KEYPOINT]
> **Appositive Clauses（同位语从句）** 用来进一步解释一个抽象名词（如 fact、idea、belief、possibility），这种结构在 Academic Writing 中十分正式，也是 SWT 高分表达的重要组成部分。

:::columns

:::column

### Common Nouns

- fact
- idea
- belief
- possibility
- assumption

:::

:::column

### Usually Followed by

- that
- whether

:::

:::

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Appositive Clause Example

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Original Sentences**
>
> Renewable energy can reduce carbon emissions.
>
> This fact has been confirmed by numerous scientific studies.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]

> [!SUCCESS]
> **Academic Combination**
>
> The **fact that renewable energy can reduce carbon emissions** has been confirmed by numerous scientific studies.

---

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Another Example**
>
> The **belief that early childhood education has a lasting impact on cognitive development** is widely accepted among educational researchers.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!TIP]
> Don't confuse an appositive clause with a relative clause.
>
> ✔ The fact **that...**
>
> ✘ The fact **which...**

> # Technique 5
>
> ## Participial Phrase

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!KEYPOINT]
> **Participial Phrases（分词短语）** 可以将一个完整句子压缩成一个短语，使句子更加紧凑、更具 Academic Style。

:::columns

:::column

### Present Participle

- doing

表示主动

:::

:::column

### Past Participle

- done

表示被动

:::

:::

left: Created by Chi - Lofty

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Participial Phrase Example

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Original Sentences**
>
> Governments are investing heavily in renewable energy.
>
> This investment creates new employment opportunities and stimulates economic growth.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]

> [!SUCCESS]
> **Academic Combination**
>
> Governments are investing heavily in renewable energy, **creating new employment opportunities and stimulating economic growth.**

> # Technique 7
>
> ## Passive Voice

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!KEYPOINT]
> **Passive Voice（被动语态）** 能够突出动作本身，而不是执行者，因此广泛应用于 Academic Writing、Research Papers 和 SWT。

:::columns

:::column

### When to Use

- 强调结果
- 强调事实
- 执行者不重要
- 保持正式语气

:::

:::column

### Structure

Subject

↓

be + Past Participle

↓

(Optional) by + Agent

:::

:::

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Passive Voice Example

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Original Sentences**
>
> Scientists developed a highly effective vaccine.
>
> The vaccine significantly reduced infection rates worldwide.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]

> [!SUCCESS]
> **Academic Combination**
>
> A highly effective vaccine **was developed by scientists**, significantly reducing infection rates worldwide.

---

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Another Example**
>
> More than 70% of renewable electricity **is generated from** wind and solar energy in several European countries.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!TIP]
> Passive voice is particularly useful when the performer of an action is obvious or unimportant.

>
> ## Prepositional Phrase

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!KEYPOINT]
> **Prepositional Phrases（介词短语）** 能够将完整从句压缩成更简洁的表达，使句子更加正式、紧凑且具有学术风格。

:::columns

:::column

### Common Expressions

- due to
- because of
- owing to
- as a result of
- in addition to

:::

:::column

### Academic Alternatives

- in response to
- with regard to
- in terms of
- in light of
- on the basis of

:::

:::

> # Technique 9
>
> ## Nominalisation

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!KEYPOINT]
> **Nominalisation（名词化）** 是 Academic Writing 最重要的技巧之一，它将动词或形容词转换成名词，使表达更加正式、客观。

:::columns

:::column

### Verb → Noun

- develop → development
- decide → decision
- improve → improvement
- analyse → analysis

:::

:::column

### Adjective → Noun

- efficient → efficiency
- responsible → responsibility
- available → availability
- equal → equality

:::

:::

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Nominalisation Example

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Original Sentences**
>
> Governments invest heavily in renewable energy.
>
> This improves environmental sustainability.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]

> [!SUCCESS]
> **Academic Combination**
>
> Government **investment** in renewable energy contributes significantly to environmental **sustainability**.

---

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Another Example**
>
> The rapid **development** of artificial intelligence has accelerated technological **innovation** across numerous industries.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!TIP]
> High-scoring SWT responses usually contain several nominalised expressions rather than relying on simple verbs.

> # Technique 10
>
> ## Reduction

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!KEYPOINT]
> **Reduction（省略）** 指在不改变原意的情况下省略重复信息，使句子更加紧凑，是 SWT 常见的压缩技巧。

:::columns

:::column

### Common Reductions

- who are → -ing
- which are → -ing
- that are → -ing

:::

:::column

### Examples

- students studying abroad
- policies introduced last year
- people living in cities

:::

:::

left: Created by Chi -Lofty
right: {{page}}

> # Reduction Example

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Original Sentence**
>
> Students who study abroad usually develop stronger intercultural communication skills and become more independent.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]

> [!SUCCESS]
> **Academic Combination**
>
> Students **studying abroad** usually develop stronger intercultural communication skills and become more independent.

---

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]

> # Technique 11
>
> ## Cause & Effect

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!KEYPOINT]
> **Cause & Effect（因果关系）** 是 SWT 最常见的逻辑关系之一，用于说明事件发生的原因及其产生的结果，使 Summary 更具逻辑性。

:::columns

:::column

### Cause

- because of
- due to
- owing to
- as a result of
- stem from
- result from

:::

:::column

### Effect

- therefore
- thus
- consequently
- hence
- as a result
- lead to
- contribute to

:::

:::

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Cause & Effect Example

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Original Sentences**
>
> Rapid urbanisation has significantly increased the demand for energy.
>
> Higher energy consumption has accelerated carbon emissions.
>
> Increased carbon emissions have contributed to global climate change.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]

> [!SUCCESS]
> **Academic Combination**
>
> Rapid urbanisation has significantly increased energy consumption, **which has consequently accelerated carbon emissions, contributing to global climate change.**

---

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Another Example**
>
> Continuous investment in scientific research has stimulated technological innovation, **thereby improving productivity and promoting long-term economic growth.**

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!TIP]
> Instead of simply using "because", combine multiple cause-and-effect relationships naturally within one sentence.

:::column

### Comparison

- likewise
- in the same way
- equally

:::

:::column

- while
- however
- by contrast
- in contrast
- on the contrary

:::

:::

left: Created by Chi - Lofty

- despite

- however
- even so

:::

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Technique 14
>
> ## Parallel Structure

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!KEYPOINT]
> **Parallel Structure（平行结构）** 是 Academic English 中的重要修辞方式。保持相同语法结构可以增强句子的逻辑性、节奏感和可读性。

:::columns

:::column

### Common Patterns

- to do, to do, to do
- doing, doing, doing
- noun, noun, noun

:::

:::column

### Benefits

- Better rhythm
- Better clarity
- More academic
- Easier to read

:::

:::

> # Parallel Structure Example

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Technique 15
>
> ## Reporting Verbs

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!KEYPOINT]
> **Reporting Verbs（报道动词）** 用于概括作者、研究者或文章的观点，而不是机械地使用 **say** 或 **tell**。这是 Academic Writing 最典型的特征之一。

:::columns

:::column

### Neutral

- states
- says
- reports
- describes
- explains

:::

:::column

### Academic

- argues
- claims
- suggests
- demonstrates
- reveals
- highlights
- emphasizes
- indicates
- proposes
- concludes

:::

:::

[footer]
left: Created by Chi - Lofty
right: {{page}}

<!-- slide -->

> [!ANIMATE:highlight]
> # Reporting Verbs Example

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Original Sentences**
>
> The author says that renewable energy is becoming increasingly important.
>
> The author also says that governments should invest more in clean technologies.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]

> [!SUCCESS]
> **Academic Combination**
>
> The author **argues** that renewable energy is becoming increasingly important and **emphasizes** the need for greater governmental investment in clean technologies.

---

> [!ANIMATE:slide-up]

> [!NOTE]
> **Another Example**
>
> The report **reveals** that global temperatures have continued to rise, while recent evidence **suggests** that immediate action is essential.

---

> [!ANIMATE:zoom-in]
> [!TIP]
> Different reporting verbs express different degrees of certainty.
>
> - **states** → 客观陈述
> - **suggests** → 提出观点
> - **argues** → 强调论证
> - **demonstrates** → 有证据证明
> - **concludes** → 最终结论
